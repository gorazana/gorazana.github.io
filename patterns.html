Compose, don’t duplicate

Start from a real application.

These build patterns show sensible boundaries and optional combinations. They are not installable recipes or promises of feature availability.

An API product

A native Goravel API with bounded errors, explicit client authorization and measurable usage. Select only the adapters the service actually needs.

API KitDefenderMeteringBillingOutboxObservability

API Kit is a contract/tooling layer, not another router. Metering does not authorize access or prove payment.

A multi-tenant application

Separate account identity, admission, workspace data, commercial entitlements and the operator experience.

TenancyDefenderAccounts (UMS)FeaturesLicensingBillingGorazine

An email domain, paid subscription or selected workspace never substitutes for tenant admission.

A publishing platform

Govern content through editorial review, publishing channels, search and safe asset delivery.

CMSMediaContent SafetySearchLocalizationWorkflows

CMS publication and uploaded-file safety are independently enforced; sanitization cannot replace output escaping.

Commerce & fulfillment

Compose catalog, stock, orders, payment evidence, invoicing and dispatch without a universal store database.

CommerceInventoryBillingInvoicingLogisticsWebhooks

Browser success redirects and carrier observations do not prove settlement or stock changes.

An education system

A school-focused application with distinct admissions, learner records, programmes, assessment and shared services.

SchoolAdmissionsLearningAssessmentsPeopleCommunicationsRecords (DMS)

Curricula, statutory reporting, safeguarding and local policy require explicit application profiles.

Offline field operations

Mobile or desktop workflows with bounded offline commands, reconciled state and explicit recovery.

SyncGeospatialFormsAssetsDevicesOutbox

This is an integration pattern, not a claim that a Wails/mobile starter or conflict-free business synchronization already ships.

A collaborative workspace

Document-centered collaboration with shared editing, governed records and visible change history.

Records (DMS)CollaborationRealtimeHistoryActivityAudit

Undoable editor history is separate from immutable decision records; a CRDT cannot merge every business operation.

Manufacturing & logistics

Work orders, material movements, assets and shipment evidence supported by domain-owned quality controls.

ManufacturingInventoryProcurementAssetsLogisticsAccounting

Safety, physical verification, country accounting and production-specific quality rules remain outside generic scaffolding.

People & recruitment

Keep candidate applications, employment records, time, payroll and account access distinct.

RecruitmentPeopleTimekeepingPayrollAccounts (UMS)Privacy

Hiring decisions and jurisdictional pay rules need review; no universal legal or payroll certification is implied.

A service or nonprofit portal

Human-led requests, evidence, approvals, funding and measured impact with least-privilege access.

CasesFormsDirectoryRecords (DMS)FundraisingImpactPrivacy

Case evidence and financial grants require domain-specific authority, retention and conflict-of-interest policies.
