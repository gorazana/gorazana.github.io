Research and architecture

Wider thinking.
Sharper boundaries.

The expansion adds distinct capabilities, not a second framework. We preserve the original research and separate external facts from our proposed engineering.

Fourteen additions, with a reason to exist

Six packages address realtime delivery, shared editing, geographic data, usage metering, bounded rules and reusable form definitions. Seven modules address privacy requests, devices, shipments, manufacturing, human-led cases, recruitment and governed experiments. Testbench is developer tooling - not another production runtime.

RealtimeCollaborationGeospatialMeteringRulesFormsPrivacyDevicesLogisticsManufacturingCasesRecruitmentExperimentsTestbench

Strengthen existing components first

  • API Kit: document synchronous and asynchronous contracts; stable errors, idempotency and deprecation policies must be explicit.
  • Outbox, Workflows and Sync: distinguish event envelopes, delivery receipts, business transitions and offline conflicts; no universal exactly-once claim.
  • CMS, Media and Records: separate public editorial content, files and governed business documents; use one set of current authorization checks.
  • Billing, Metering and Licensing: measurement, payment evidence and entitlements have different authorities and correction lifecycles.
  • Create and Package Skeleton: expose compatibility, source ownership, failed-step recovery and a dry-run before any generated application is changed.
  • Observability and Testbench: record exact versions, tested adapters, skipped cases and restore evidence; fixture success is not production certification.

Do not add a framework inside the framework

Logging, native HTTP, routing, validation, queues, scheduling and storage remain Goravel-owned services. Reusable capabilities should use those contracts and declare their adapter boundaries. Healthcare records, regulated financial workflows, legal retention rules and specialized industrial control remain explicit application profiles until separately researched and tested.
